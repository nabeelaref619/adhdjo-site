Contact the community

The existing community channel is @adhdjo on Instagram. Use it for website feedback, a public listing correction or a community recommendation.

Help us check a correction

Include the listed name, what needs correcting, and a link to the provider's official public page or clinic website. Tell us if a location or contact method has changed. A public source helps distinguish people who share similar names.

Please do not send medical records, identity documents, payment details or private information about another person. We do not provide consultations, appointment bookings or personal medical advice through this website.

Looking for an appointment?

Contact the clinic or professional directly using a current official source. Ask about availability, fees, languages and which age groups they see. A community listing is a starting point and is not confirmation that the professional can meet your particular needs.

Urgent help

This community channel is not monitored as an emergency service. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare service directly.
