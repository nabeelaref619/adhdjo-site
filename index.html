Finding your next step

A recommendation can help you start a search, but it cannot tell you whether a professional is right for your situation. Our directory keeps names submitted by the community separate from independently sourced provider details. Before booking, confirm the person's identity, qualifications, location, fees and availability directly with their clinic.

Use the directory carefully

Search for a name, then use a current official clinic or provider page to confirm details. A community listing does not establish ADHD expertise or a licence.

Prepare for a first appointment

Write down what you want help with and the questions you want answered. Ask the clinic about the appointment process before sharing personal records.

Plan your questions →

Check what you read online

Look for an identifiable author, a clear source and a recent review date. Personal stories offer perspective; a qualified professional can help you understand your own circumstances.
