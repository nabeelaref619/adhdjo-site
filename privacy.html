Privacy

A simple website with clear controls over what stays in your browser.

Tool answers and browser controls

The website code does not transmit task or routine text or question choices, or save these answers in site-managed storage. Your browser may retain or restore form entries through autocomplete or session restoration, including after a reload or reopening a tab. Use Clear this tool before leaving a shared device and manage saved form and session data in your browser settings. Autocomplete is disabled where appropriate, but browsers may still restore entries. Printing is optional. Do not paste private health records into these tools.

Optional local settings

A manual language choice can be remembered on this device. If you enable learning mode, only an enabled flag and completed mission IDs are saved locally. There are no health scores, symptom answers, streak tracking or accounts. You can reset progress or disable the mode.

Hosting and external links

Ordinary web requests reach GitHub Pages, including your IP address and browser information. Provider, Instagram and source links open other services under their own policies. They are links, not embedded trackers. This website uses system fonts. The AdSense ownership meta tag and ads.txt do not serve ads on their own; advertising services and choices are described separately below.

Advertising and privacy choices

Selected information pages use Google AdSense to support this website. Advertising is subject to Google approval and may not be shown on every visit. Ads are labelled and do not represent a medical recommendation or an endorsement by ADHDJO. Interactive tools and the support directory do not load advertising scripts.

We configure ad requests as non-personalized and disable ad personalization through Google's publisher privacy setting. We do not send tool answers, appointment notes, task text, routine text or learning progress to advertising services, and do not create advertising audiences from health information.

Google and its advertising partners may receive the page URL, IP address, browser and device information, and advertising identifiers. Non-personalized ads can still use cookies or local storage for functions such as limiting repeated ads, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. They may use contextual information, including the page topic and approximate location.

Google's consent message offers consent, refusal and preference controls where European regulations apply. Use Privacy and cookie settings at the bottom of an information page or this privacy page to change or withdraw your choice when that control is available. Applicable US visitors can use Google's Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link. Depending on your choices and location, Google may show limited ads or no ads. Browser settings also let you manage cookies and stored data.

This privacy page loads the AdSense tag to make applicable privacy controls available, but contains no ad placement. We have not installed a separate visitor-analytics service or social-media tracking pixels. How Google uses information from sites that use its services · Google ad preferences.
