A practical guide to finding support

A useful next step does not have to be a large one. You might start by finding reliable background information, checking a professional's public details, or writing down questions for a clinic. This guide helps organise those steps without assessing your health.

1. Check a community recommendation

Use the directory to find a name. Treat a name match as a lead, especially when a name is transliterated differently in Arabic and English. Confirm the person's identity through an official clinic or provider page; do not assume that similarly named profiles refer to the same person.

A location in the original community list may be a country or “Online Therapy”, rather than a city. Ask where appointments take place and whether an online service can serve someone in Jordan. Community approval does not establish credentials or current practice.

When a listing has a provider-source link, read that source and look at its check date. Confirm the phone number with the clinic before calling or sharing documents. Missing contact details mean we do not have a confirmed contact method; they are not an invitation to use a guessed number.

2. Prepare your clinic questions

Before booking, ask practical questions that the clinic can answer: which age groups does the professional see, what is the appointment format, how long is it, what does it cost, and what language can be used? If you need accessibility support, ask about it in advance.

Ask how the professional's qualifications can be checked and whether the clinic provides the type of service you are seeking. A directory label alone is not evidence of specialist experience. Keep a note of the clinic's answers so you can compare practical fit without relying only on popularity.

For an appointment, you may choose to bring a short description of your concerns, what you hope to discuss and questions you do not want to forget. Ask the clinic what documents, if any, it wants and how they should be shared securely. Keep private health information off public comment threads.

3. Make follow-up easier

At the end of a conversation, ask what the next step is, who to contact with questions, and whether there are extra fees or forms. If something is unclear, ask the clinic to explain it in plain language. You can ask for time to consider your options.

If you are exploring support at school or work, start by asking about the organisation's process and the person responsible for support requests. Share only what is necessary for that conversation. This website does not establish eligibility, legal rights or a particular adjustment.

4. Read with a source in view

For clinical background, start with an established health organisation and check whether the page addresses adults, children or both. Information written for another country may explain general concepts while using a healthcare pathway that differs from Jordan's.

  • NIMH: ADHD — What You Need to Know is a general clinical information starting point.
  • NHS: ADHD in children and young people addresses younger people in a UK healthcare context.
  • NHS: ADHD in adults provides an adult-focused starting point in the same context.

These links are background reading, not an assessment or a local referral route. A checklist, video or personal story cannot establish a diagnosis. Take questions about your own circumstances to a qualified healthcare professional.

When reading other material, look for the author's identity, the evidence behind a claim and a review date. Be cautious with promises of certainty, secret solutions or advice to change care without discussing it with a professional.

ابدأ بالسؤال الذي تريد الإجابة عنه: معلومات عامة، موعد، أم معرفة آلية طلب الدعم؟ عند البحث في الدليل، تحقق من هوية الشخص عبر موقع عيادته أو صفحته الرسمية، ولا تعتمد على تشابه الأسماء.

قبل الحجز، اسأل عن الفئة العمرية التي يستقبلها مقدم الخدمة، لغة الجلسة، المكان، التكلفة وطريقة التأكد من المؤهلات. سجّل أسئلتك، واطلب توضيح الخطوة التالية. تجنب مشاركة بياناتك الصحية في التعليقات العامة.

مصادر القراءة هنا معلومات عامة، وقد تختلف إجراءات الرعاية في الأردن عن الدول التي كتبت فيها. القصص الشخصية والقوائم المختصرة لا تشخّص حالتك. ناقش ظروفك مع مختص مؤهل.
