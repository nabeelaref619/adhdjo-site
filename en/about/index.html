About ADHDJO

A Jordan community resource owned by Nabil Aref.

Who is responsible for ADHDJO?

ADHDJO is owned by Nabil Aref. It began as a Jordan community resource and provides Arabic and English educational guides, practical planning tools and a source-checked service directory. Nabil handles website feedback, corrections and content enquiries.

naref@adhdjo.org · Instagram @adhdjo

What you can use this site for

Prepare questions for an appointment, compare what services say they offer, and try a small organizational aid. Guides combine cited background information with original checklists and clearly labelled fictional examples. They do not diagnose you, select a treatment or replace individualized care.

How the content was made

This revision used AI assistance for research, writing, translation and website development. Sources are linked beside the claims they support. Independent clinical review has not been completed. Review status is stated so readers can judge the limits of the material.

Read the editorial and corrections policy

Why the directory is selective

A useful listing needs an identifiable service, evidence for what it offers and a contact route. The former community name list has been replaced by entries that meet the published evidence criteria. An omitted name does not mean a professional is unqualified; it means we have not established enough information for a useful listing in this revision.

Advertising

Selected educational articles include labelled Google advertising, subject to approval and availability. An advertisement is not a recommendation of a provider, product or treatment.
