Clear information. Honest limits.

ADHDJO began as a Jordan community resource and now offers Arabic and English educational material for readers across countries.

What the website provides

An educational starting point, practical tools and a curated directory. It does not diagnose, prescribe, book appointments, verify clinical quality or promise a provider’s suitability.

How this resource is prepared

Guides are original source-based summaries and practical examples. Public-health agencies and clinical guidance are linked beside relevant content. Examples and tools are not validated treatments. Sources have been checked, but independent clinical review has not been completed.

Corrections and source updates

If information is outdated or unclear, contact the existing community channel with the page link and a public source. Do not send private health records or confidential information about another person.

For education and practical support only. This website does not diagnose ADHD or replace individualized advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Sources checked on 13 October 2026. This content has not undergone independent clinical review.

Arabic content is an editorial translation, not an official translation from the cited organizations.
