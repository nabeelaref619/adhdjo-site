Editorial policy and corrections

Who publishes this content, how it is prepared, and how to report an error.

Ownership and contact

Nabil Aref owns ADHDJO and is responsible for receiving corrections. Contact naref@adhdjo.org or @adhdjo on Instagram. This website is an educational community resource, not a healthcare provider.

Research and authorship

AI assistance was used to locate sources, draft explanations and translate the Arabic and English text. The publisher is ADHDJO. Ownership, publication and source checking should not be mistaken for a medical qualification or independent clinical review.

What counts as evidence

Clinical background is linked to public-health agencies, professional guidance or primary research. Overseas guidance is identified by its source; it does not establish Jordanian law, local medicine availability, eligibility or fees. Provider pages establish what a service says it offers, not its clinical quality.

Examples, worksheets and translations

Fictional examples and original planning worksheets are clearly described. They are not patient testimonials, diagnostic tests or validated treatment programs. Arabic text is an editorial translation prepared with AI assistance, not an official translation endorsed by the cited organizations.

Corrections

Send the page URL, the statement that needs correcting and a public primary source where possible. Do not send medical records or confidential details about another person. Reports are reviewed through the owner’s contact channels; there is no guaranteed response time. Material corrections should identify what changed and when.

Dates and verification limits

An updated date records a content revision. A directory check date records the public evidence examined on that date. Neither confirms current appointment availability. A page is described as clinically reviewed only after a qualified reviewer has actually reviewed it and agreed to be identified.
