Build a small routine

Attach a small action to a regular moment in your day.

This website does not save or send tool answers. Your browser may restore form entries. Use Clear this tool on a shared device. Print only if you choose.

How to use it

  1. Choose one routine, such as preparing tomorrow’s bag.
  2. Make the first version easy enough to repeat.
  3. A missed day is information, not failure. Adjust and restart.
