Prepare for an appointment

Gather your questions without trying to diagnose yourself.

Choose questions you want to take to the appointment

Keep personal health history, medicine notes and private examples offline or share them securely with your clinician. This tool does not ask you to enter them.

How to use it

  1. Prepare concrete examples privately, including what happened and its impact.
  2. Note available childhood information; ask the clinician if records are missing.
  3. Choose your three most important questions.
