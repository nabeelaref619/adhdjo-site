Jordan: assessment and psychiatric services

Four published routes to ask about ADHD assessment or care. Confirm the professional, ages served and the full assessment process before booking.

Amman, Jordan

Dr. Layali Abbasi Clinic

The clinic lists child/adolescent ADHD assessment tools (ages 6–18) and adult ADHD rating scales alongside psychiatric services. Ask how these are used within a comprehensive assessment.

Before paying for an assessment

Ask who will assess you, their profession and current licence, the ages they work with, and what the assessment, report and follow-up include. Confirm the full cost and language access. A test or symptom scale alone is not an ADHD diagnosis.
