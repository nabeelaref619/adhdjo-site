Your country is not listed?

A missing country does not mean care is unavailable. Start with your usual healthcare professional or local health service. Ask about referral routes, which professional can assess ADHD, and how to check their current registration with the local regulator. Confirm costs, ages served, accessibility and follow-up before booking.

Questions to ask before choosing care

Ask who will perform the assessment, their relevant training and registration, what the process includes, and how other explanations will be considered. A provider’s own service page can establish what it says it offers; it cannot establish clinical quality.
