How we check the support directory

Our inclusion criteria, what we checked on 19 November 2026, and what you still need to confirm directly.

Published by ADHDJO · Owner: Nabil Aref · Updated 19 November 2026.
AI-assisted research, drafting and translation. Independent clinical review has not been completed. How this content is prepared

This is an information check, not practitioner accreditation. “Published information checked” means we compared a listing with its source pages. It does not mean ADHDJO has verified a licence, assessed quality or recommended the service.

What qualifies for inclusion?

We select resources that publish a relevant service and a usable contact route. Jordan comes first, followed by regional and international services and directories that may help readers where they live. This is not a complete list. Entries are grouped by location and service type, not ranked by quality.

  • For ADHD assessment or care, we look for an explicit service description on the provider’s or responsible health-service website.
  • We identify psychological therapy, learning or developmental support, and other directories separately. Mentioning ADHD does not automatically make a resource a diagnostic clinic.
  • We state published age, residence and referral restrictions and identify details that need confirmation.
  • Restricted specialist services appear in a separate section, with conditions visible before the contact link.

What did we check?

On 19 November 2026, we checked published names, service descriptions, ages, location, contact routes and access restrictions. Each card links to the sources used. We use the provider, organization or public health-service website; a provider’s profile establishes what it says, not the validity of every credential it claims.

When sources disagree, we flag the difference. For example, the Philadelphia center’s main page lists ages 4–18 while an older notice says 3–18, so its card asks you to confirm age eligibility. The Maudsley national service card also records the November 2026 closure to new national referrals.

What remains unconfirmed?

  • Current practitioner or facility licences with the relevant regulator.
  • Clinical quality, outcomes, adequacy of training or suitability for your needs.
  • Current appointment availability, price, insurance cover or physical accessibility.
  • Permission to provide remote care or prescribe across borders.

We did not contact practitioners or book appointments. Research, drafting and translation were AI-assisted; independent clinical review has not been completed. The date records when we checked the source, not a guarantee that the provider updated every detail on that day.

Before relying on a listing

  1. Read the service type, age group and access conditions. Coaching, psychological support, diagnosis and prescribing are different services.
  2. Open the source and contact the service to confirm your age and location are eligible, and ask about appointments, full cost, the report and follow-up.
  3. Ask for the treating professional’s name, profession and licence number, then check current status with the appropriate regulator.
  4. In Jordan, use our guide to the assessment route and checking credentials before paying.

Professional association membership or inclusion in another directory does not establish a current licence. An online appointment option also does not establish permission to treat patients in every country.

How are suggested names handled?

A community suggestion is a research lead, not evidence of qualifications or quality. We do not publish a name without a suitable source describing the service and a contact route. Insufficient public information is a reason for omission, not a judgment that a practitioner is unqualified. This edition contains 18 resources, including six Jordan options and two restricted international specialist referral services.

Report a correction or suggest an update

Send the listing link, the detail to correct and a current source URL to naref@adhdjo.org. Do not send medical records or identifying patient information. We will compare the correction with the source before changing the listing; a suggestion does not guarantee inclusion.

Service details can change after the review date. Confirm them directly each time you book.
