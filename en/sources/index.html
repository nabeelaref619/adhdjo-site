How content is researched and corrected

Understand ADHD

Start with a broad explanation, then choose an age-specific source.

ADHD: What You Need to Know

NIMH · United States

A starting point for symptoms, assessment and the broad treatment picture. It helps explain clinical background; it does not select treatment for an individual.

ADHD in Adults: 4 Things to Know

NIMH · United States

Useful for adult recognition, childhood history, overlooked symptoms and treatment options. Read this alongside the appointment guide if you are seeking an adult assessment.

ADHD in Children

CDC · United States

Explains the condition, possible contributing factors and its effect on childhood development and daily life. US service information requires local checking before use in Jordan.

Symptoms of ADHD

CDC · United States

Describes different symptom patterns. A list of familiar experiences can help you form questions; it cannot establish a diagnosis.

ADHD overview

MedlinePlus / National Library of Medicine · United States

A public-health overview with links for understanding symptoms, diagnosis and treatment across life stages.

Assessment and appointment preparation

Use these sources to ask what an assessment includes and how its conclusion will be explained.

Diagnosing ADHD

CDC · United States

Explains why assessment has several steps, considers more than one setting and checks other explanations. Its US referral and evaluation arrangements are not Jordanian entitlements.

ADHD Screening

MedlinePlus / National Library of Medicine · United States

Explains questionnaires, health history and additional checks that may be needed. No single screening result settles the diagnosis.

The adult ADHD assessment quality assurance standard

UK Adult ADHD Network / Frontiers in Psychiatry · United Kingdom

A 2024 professional consensus standard for detailed adult assessment and meaningful feedback, including missing or conflicting information. Useful for questions about assessment quality; it is not Jordanian law.

ADHD: diagnosis and management — NG87

NICE · United Kingdom

Clinical recommendations on assessment, support and shared treatment planning. UK clinical guidance does not establish Jordanian service routes or professional permissions.

Recommendation excerpts checked on 19 November 2026; a complete-page recheck remains pending.

Talking with a healthcare provider about mental health

NIMH · United States

Supports preparing questions and medicine lists and describing specific concerns. Ask your own clinic about consent and confidentiality; this page does not establish local rules.

Treatment questions and medicine safety

These references support informed questions for a qualified clinician. They do not choose a medicine, dose or treatment for you.

Treatment of ADHD

CDC · United States

Explains how child treatment recommendations vary by age, including parent training and medication. The relevant options still need an individual care discussion.

Mental Health Medications

NIMH · United States

Background on clinician supervision, differences in response and medicine safety. It does not verify Jordanian approvals, availability, price or a suitable dose for you.

Psychotherapies

NIMH · United States

General questions about finding a suitable therapy and evaluating progress. ADHD-specific treatment claims also need an ADHD source; this is not evidence that every therapy treats ADHD.

Safe use of prescription stimulants

FDA · United States

A US regulator’s safety notice about misuse, addiction and sharing prescription stimulants. This is safety background, not a list of medicines licensed or stocked in Jordan.

ADHD and Complementary Health Approaches

NCCIH · United States

Reviews the evidence and its limits for complementary approaches. Useful when discussing a product or programme; uncertain evidence should not become a promise of benefit or cure.

Support at home, school and work

Practical ideas need to fit the person and setting. Check local school and employment procedures separately.

Parent Training in Behavior Management

CDC · United States

Explains structured parent training and questions about programme delivery. Our home–school notes are original planning aids, not a substitute for such a programme.

ADHD in the Classroom

CDC · United States

Describes classroom support and organizational approaches. References to US IEP or 504 arrangements must not be presented as the school process or legal entitlement in Jordan.

ADHD in children and young people

NHS · United Kingdom

Connects symptoms, assessment, home and school support. UK referral routes and school roles need separate local confirmation.

ADHD in adults

NHS · United Kingdom

Adult information with examples of support at work and in education. A practical support request does not, by itself, establish a particular legal entitlement in Jordan.

Sleep and wider wellbeing

These sources provide general health information alongside ADHD-specific guidance.

Insomnia

NHS · United Kingdom

Explains sleep problems, general habits and when to seek clinical advice. Supports the sleep guide’s basic information; it does not validate our routine builder as treatment.

Sleep problems

NHS Every Mind Matters · United Kingdom

Further general sleep support and help-seeking advice. Use it to prepare a conversation about persistent problems and the constraints in your daily life.

Caring for Your Mental Health

NIMH · United States

General self-care and help-seeking information. It provides background for manageable goals without claiming that an organizational worksheet treats ADHD.

Suicide: questions and answers

World Health Organization · International

Background for recognizing the need for urgent help and involving a trusted person. It does not identify local emergency numbers or provide crisis care through this website.

Checking professional and facility information in Jordan

Official routes to investigate registration questions. A public-source check is not a clinical quality rating.

Licensing Directorate responsibilities

Jordan Ministry of Health · Jordan

The directorate lists issuing statements about the validity and current status of practice permits among its responsibilities. A route for asking how to verify a permit; no named clinician’s current licence is established here.

Licensed clinics, centres and health institutions

Jordan Ministry of Health · Jordan

An official index of facility categories, including specialist medicine and clinical psychology/counselling centres. A facility entry is not proof of every clinician’s ADHD expertise or appointment availability.

Provider-specific service claims, contact routes and access restrictions are linked on the directory entries. Read the directory method to understand what was checked.
