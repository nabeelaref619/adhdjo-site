Privacy

A simple website with clear controls over what stays in your browser.

Tool answers and browser controls

The website code does not transmit task or routine text or question choices, or save these answers in site-managed storage. Your browser may retain or restore form entries through autocomplete or session restoration, including after a reload or reopening a tab. Use Clear this tool before leaving a shared device and manage saved form and session data in your browser settings. Autocomplete is disabled where appropriate, but browsers may still restore entries. Printing is optional. Do not paste private health records into these tools.

Optional local settings

A manual language choice can be remembered on this device. If you enable learning mode, only an enabled flag and completed mission IDs are saved locally. There are no health scores, symptom answers, streak tracking or accounts. You can reset progress or disable the mode.

Hosting and external links

Ordinary web requests reach GitHub Pages, including your IP address and browser information. Provider, Instagram and source links open other services under their own policies. They are links, not embedded trackers. This version uses system fonts and has no analytics, advertising script, tracking pixel or site-created cookie. The AdSense ownership meta tag and ads.txt do not serve ads.

Before any future advertising

Advertising and applicable consent controls will be reviewed separately before ad code is enabled. The privacy notice will be updated to reflect the actual services. Tool content and health information will not be used for personalized advertising audiences.
