About adhdjo

adhdjo is a community resource focused on finding support and sharing experiences around ADHD in Jordan. This website connects the existing @adhdjo Instagram community with a directory and practical reading guides.

What this site provides

We offer a starting point for a personal search, not a clinical assessment or a booking service. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, verify licensing, or guarantee any provider's suitability. A professional's current official page is the appropriate source for their qualifications and contact details.

How community listings work

The directory reads the original published community Sheet. Only rows whose status is explicitly “Approved” can appear. A blank, pending or rejected status does not qualify. Sample records and browser-only edits are not public listings.

Approval means a name has been accepted into the community list. It does not mean a clinical credential, ADHD specialty or quality of care has been independently verified. Reported locations come from the community list and may need updating.

The original list includes incomplete and uncertain details. We preserve approved names while withholding its unconfirmed role descriptions, phone numbers and vetting notes. When an official provider source has been checked, the website can display separately sourced details with a source link and check date. No phone or WhatsApp link appears without sourced contact information; WhatsApp also needs explicit confirmation that the provider offers it.

Corrections and recommendations

Use the community contact channel to request a correction or recommend a professional. Include a public official provider or clinic link. Do not send medical records or someone else's private details. Listing corrections need review before they appear.

Editorial approach

Our practical guides focus on questions to ask and ways to evaluate information. We link clinical background reading to established public health sources. Community stories are labelled as experiences. Where details are uncertain, we say so.

Website guidance reviewed: 12 October 2026. Provider details, when available, carry their own check dates.
